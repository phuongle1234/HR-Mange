# Business Overview

## Purpose
Define the business context for the Employee Management System.

## Business Goal
Provide a controlled system for managing employee information. Users authenticate, receive permission-based access, and perform employee operations according to approved rules.

## Primary Capabilities
- Authenticate users.
- Authorize protected operations.
- List employees.
- View employee details.
- Create employees.
- Update employees.
- Delete employees.
- Change own password.
- Request password reset instructions.
- Record audit logs for important employee changes.

## Business Principles
- Protected operations require authentication.
- Employee operations require explicit permissions.
- Important employee changes require audit logs.
- Security-sensitive data must not be exposed.
- Unapproved employee rules remain pending rather than assumed.

## Current Business Ambiguities
- Employee fields are not fully approved.
- Department relationship is blocked.
- Employee status model is not approved.
- Delete behavior is not approved.
- Password policy is not approved.
